Our story

It started on a camp stove up the Ottawa Valley

Our founder roasted coffee in a hand-cranked drum over a propane burner on long weekends, and friends kept asking for bags to take home. We still roast the way we started: small batches, one coffee at a time, tasting every roast before it ships.

  • Every coffee gets its own roast profile
  • Plain-English tasting notes and a brew guide on every bag
  • A 30-day satisfaction promise: love it or we’ll make it right
