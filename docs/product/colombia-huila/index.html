Description

Huila’s high-altitude farms grow some of Colombia’s sweetest coffee. This lot is clean and balanced, with a caramel body and a crisp apple note on the finish that keeps every sip interesting.

It works with almost any brewer. If you’re moving up from grocery-store coffee and want to taste the difference without anything too wild, this is the bag.

Roaster’s note: “I once found a bag of this behind the oatmeal at eleven weeks old. Don’t do that. At two weeks it’s all apple and caramel.” (Owen, head roaster)

Tasting notes

Red apple, caramel, cocoa.
